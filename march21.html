How was Ezra supposed to fall for me when I never even talked to him. I was just some background character in his life, not worthy of his love. I just feel so stupid after all of this!

You know what? Fuck this other people shit. I don’t deserve anyone, including my new friends. Well, they don’t ever have to worry about me again. I won’t be bothering them anymore.
