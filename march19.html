March 19th

I’ve been hanging out with Vega, Finn, and Rowan some more this last month. We meet up at school whenever we have a free moment, usually grabbing lunch together in between classes.

Finn also comes and harasses me while I’m at work. I sorta tell him that I hate it, but it’s time I actually cherish. He cares enough about me to come by and entertain me while I’m at my boring job.

Don’t get me wrong, I love my job and my boss. He’s been very good to me. But with how slow the shop is on most days and how I’m either trapped with my boss or Logan, I’m happy to have Finn there to chat with while I’m stocking shelves or sitting around, waiting for people to come in and buy from us.

Finn’s also agreed to come with me to group. I know, crazy. Turns out that, while I clocked him as autistic right away, I completely missed that he was a trans guy AND bisexual. So, he’s coming with me tomorrow. Maybe it’d be good for him. He might be dealing with his own demons I don’t know about.

 Cedar
