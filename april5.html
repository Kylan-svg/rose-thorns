April 5th

Oh fuck. Oh fuck! Fuck! Fuck!! Fuck!!! Fuck!!!

I like Finn! What the hell am I doing?! I JUST fucking said that I would never let myself fall for someone again! But here I am, already obsessed with him.

We went to the arcade together, just the two of us. This was the first time we’d hung out alone, aside from going to group together. And I was looking at him, and it all hit me at once! He’s so wonderful and here I am, already falling for him.

During group tonight, I was staring at him and got so lost in him that I didn’t even hear what he had to say. Why do I always fall so hard for people? It’s so infuriating!

Not like Finn could ever have feelings for me, so I just have to pretend like he isn’t the most incredible person I’ve ever met and that I don’t have feelings for him. Yeah, I can do this. I would hate to ruin the friendship we have together. He’s so important to me already, I could never let him go…

God he’s so cute, I get fucking cute aggression when I look at him.

What the fuck am I supposed to do?

 Cedar
